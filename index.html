Austin Rainbow Girls:
Leaders, Friends, Citizens.

Find your voice, make real friends, and learn to lead. Rainbow is for girls ages 11–20 who want to have fun and make a difference.

What You'll Do in Rainbow

👑

Build Leaders

Hold an office, run meetings, and plan service projects. You'll get comfortable speaking in front of a group and build confidence that lasts.

🤝

Serve Our Community

Service is at the heart of Rainbow. Together we volunteer and lead projects that make a real difference right here at home.

🌈

Create Sisterhood

Make friends for life, in Austin, across Texas, and around the world. Expect fun activities, trips, and a safe, supportive place to be yourself.

Questions? We've Got Answers

Tap a question to see the answer. Don't see yours? Email us and we'll help.

Who can join?

Girls ages 11–20 can become Rainbow members, and you can join at any time of the year. Girls ages 8–10 can start in our Pledge program. When a Pledge turns 11, she can petition to become a member.

To join, fill out the application (download it below) and have a parent or guardian sign it. A recommendation from a member or adult in the Masonic family is welcome but not required.

Do I need a Masonic relative or sponsor to join?

No. Any girl in the right age range is welcome. You don’t need a family member in a Masonic organization.

What does it cost?

There’s a one-time joining fee and a small yearly fee. Email us for the current amounts. Some events have their own costs, and we’ll always let you know ahead of time.

How much time does it take?

We meet twice a month on Wednesday evenings, plus occasional events like service projects and social activities.

Who supervises the girls?

Our Mother Advisor leads the assembly with an Advisory Board of adult volunteers from the Masonic family, including the Order of the Eastern Star. All adult leaders are background-checked and complete youth-protection training. There’s always more than one adult present at meetings and events. Parents are always welcome, and we encourage them to take part alongside their daughters.

Is Rainbow a religious group?

Rainbow asks members to believe in a Supreme Being, and it welcomes girls of every faith. It isn’t tied to any one church or religion.

Do I have to live in Leander?

No. Girls from nearby towns like Cedar Park, Liberty Hill, and Round Rock are welcome too. Many girls join the assembly closest to home, and anyone is welcome to visit.

Ready to Start Your Rainbow Journey?

Joining is easy! Rainbow is for girls ages 11–20, and girls ages 8–10 can start in our Pledge program. If you're ready to laugh, learn, and lead, we'd love to meet you.
